The Inaugural Plenary: IHSC Begins Its Work

Pleno Perdana: IHSC Memulai Kerjanya

NewsKabar IHSC EditorialRedaksi IHSC 5 June 20265 Juni 2026

On 5 June 2026, the Indonesian Humanitarian Study Center (IHSC) held its inaugural plenary session via Zoom, marking the formal start of operations for Indonesia's newest humanitarian study center.

Pada 5 Juni 2026, Indonesian Humanitarian Study Center (IHSC) menggelar pleno perdana via Zoom, menandai dimulainya operasi pusat studi kemanusiaan terbaru Indonesia.

The plenary brought together all 25 officers — the five academic field coordinators, two bureau heads, the advisory board, and the center's leadership — to align on work frameworks, program priorities, and the roadmap for the 2026–2031 term.

Pleno ini mempertemukan seluruh 25 pengurus — lima koordinator bidang akademik, dua kepala biro, dewan penasihat, dan pimpinan pusat — untuk menyelaraskan kerangka kerja, prioritas program, dan peta jalan masa jabatan 2026–2031.

A new kind of humanitarian institution

Institusi kemanusiaan jenis baru

Unlike a research institute housed in a single discipline, IHSC is deliberately interdisciplinary — spanning health sciences, law, ethics, disaster management, and climate science — and deliberately grounded in the Islamic and Nusantara intellectual traditions of Nahdlatul Ulama.

Berbeda dari lembaga riset yang berbasis pada satu disiplin, IHSC sengaja bersifat interdisipliner — mencakup ilmu kesehatan, hukum, etika, manajemen bencana, dan ilmu iklim — dan sengaja berakar pada tradisi intelektual Islam dan Nusantara milik Nahdlatul Ulama.

"Knowledge, for real action."

"Pengetahuan, untuk aksi nyata."

Next steps

Langkah selanjutnya

Each of the five academic fields is finalizing its work framework. Priority outputs include the launch of a regular journal discussion, an IHL training module, and the first research brief on community-based preparedness.

Masing-masing dari lima bidang akademik sedang memfinalisasi kerangka kerjanya. Luaran prioritas mencakup peluncuran diskusi jurnal rutin, modul pelatihan IHL, dan research brief pertama tentang kesiapsiagaan berbasis masyarakat.
