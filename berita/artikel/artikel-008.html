Three Months Under Haze: Indonesia’s 2026 Fires and the Right to Clean Air

Kabut asap tiga bulan: karhutla 2026 dan hak warga atas udara bersih

EmergencyDarurat IHSC EditorialRedaksi IHSC 16 October 202616 Oktober 2026

The forest and land fires that have swept Indonesia since July 2026 are not over. As of 13 October, the National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB) recorded 663.61 hectares still under response across six priority provinces: Riau, Jambi, South Sumatra, West Kalimantan, Central Kalimantan, and South Kalimantan. The government has deployed 60,177 personnel, supported by water bombing and weather modification. With natural rain not expected to spread until the second ten days of November, the Forestry Minister has asked all parties to stay on alert for roughly another month.

Kebakaran hutan dan lahan (karhutla) yang melanda Indonesia sejak Juli 2026 belum selesai. Per 13 Oktober, Badan Nasional Penanggulangan Bencana (BNPB) mencatat 663,61 hektare masih dalam penanganan di enam provinsi prioritas: Riau, Jambi, Sumatera Selatan, Kalimantan Barat, Kalimantan Tengah, dan Kalimantan Selatan. Pemerintah mengerahkan 60.177 personel gabungan, ditambah water bombing dan operasi modifikasi cuaca. Karena hujan alami baru diperkirakan merata pada dasarian kedua November, Menteri Kehutanan meminta semua pihak siaga sekitar satu bulan lagi.

The human cost is measurable. The Health Ministry recorded 174,694 cases of acute respiratory infection between 18 August and 21 September 2026, 40,363 of them among children under five. In South Sumatra alone, the provincial health office counted 52,483 cases in September, up 11.62 percent from August. Four people died while trapped fighting the flames in Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park, three of them park staff, and a resident of Samarinda died with burns covering 60 percent of his body. In Pekanbaru, Jambi, and East Kutai, schooling now follows air quality: distance learning or limited classroom hours.

Dampaknya terukur. Kementerian Kesehatan mencatat 174.694 kasus infeksi saluran pernapasan akut (ISPA) pada 18 Agustus sampai 21 September 2026, dan 40.363 di antaranya dialami balita. Di Sumatera Selatan, Dinas Kesehatan mencatat 52.483 kasus sepanjang September, naik 11,62 persen dari Agustus. Empat orang tewas terjebak saat memadamkan api di Taman Nasional Bromo Tengger Semeru, tiga di antaranya petugas taman nasional, dan seorang warga Samarinda meninggal dengan luka bakar 60 persen. Di Pekanbaru, Jambi, dan Kutai Timur, kegiatan belajar ditentukan mutu udara: pembelajaran jarak jauh atau tatap muka terbatas.

Here the fires stop being merely an environmental disaster. Indonesia’s National Human Rights Commission (Komnas HAM) has received nine complaints from residents of Kalimantan and Sumatra, mainly concerning health, access to education, and work, and is reviewing fire prevention governance through a human rights lens: the right to a good and healthy environment, and victims’ right to recovery. Four civil society and indigenous groups have sued the government, naming the President, the national police chief, and the West Kalimantan governor, at the Pontianak District Court. They demand full environmental restoration, stronger fire response, and accountability for the health care costs borne by affected residents. The first hearing on 7 October was postponed to 21 October.

Di titik ini karhutla berhenti menjadi sekadar bencana lingkungan. Komnas HAM menerima sembilan aduan warga Kalimantan dan Sumatera, terutama soal kesehatan, akses pendidikan, dan pekerjaan, lalu mengkaji tata kelola pencegahan karhutla dari sudut hak asasi manusia: hak atas lingkungan hidup yang baik dan sehat, serta hak korban atas pemulihan. Empat kelompok masyarakat sipil dan masyarakat adat menggugat pemerintah, termasuk Presiden, Kapolri, dan Gubernur Kalimantan Barat, ke Pengadilan Negeri Pontianak. Mereka menuntut pemulihan lingkungan, penguatan penanganan kebakaran, dan pertanggungjawaban biaya layanan kesehatan warga terdampak. Sidang perdana 7 Oktober ditunda ke 21 Oktober.

Public attention is shifting from suppression to justice. The Tanpa Kolusi Coalition found that about 40 percent of this year’s hotspots lie inside plantation concessions and forest utilisation permits. Burned area from January to July 2026 reached 202,004.30 hectares, up 69 percent from the same period in 2025. Komnas HAM is pressing for transparency in law enforcement against both individuals and corporations, while the Ombudsman has asked local governments to set official thresholds for smoke density and visibility as an early warning for residents.

Perhatian publik kini mengarah pada keadilan, bukan hanya pemadaman. Koalisi Tanpa Kolusi mencatat sekitar 40 persen titik panas tahun ini berada di area hak guna usaha dan izin pemanfaatan hutan. Luas karhutla Januari sampai Juli 2026 mencapai 202.004,30 hektare, naik 69 persen dibanding periode yang sama pada 2025. Komnas HAM mendorong keterbukaan penegakan hukum terhadap warga maupun korporasi, sedangkan Ombudsman meminta pemerintah daerah menetapkan skala resmi ketebalan asap dan jarak pandang sebagai peringatan dini bagi warga.

The Humanitarian Study Center (PSH) IHSC at IIK NU Tuban holds that a humanitarian response does not end with firefighting. What must be restored is people’s health, children’s continued education, livelihoods, and ecology, especially peatlands that need decades to recover. Until then, clean air remains a right to be fought for, not a service that happens to be available.

Pusat Studi Humanitarian (PSH) IHSC IIK NU Tuban menilai respons kemanusiaan tidak berhenti pada pemadaman. Yang harus dipulihkan adalah kesehatan warga, keberlanjutan pendidikan anak, mata pencaharian, dan ekologi, terutama lahan gambut yang butuh puluhan tahun untuk kembali seperti semula. Selama itu, udara bersih adalah hak yang masih diperjuangkan, bukan layanan yang kebetulan tersedia.
