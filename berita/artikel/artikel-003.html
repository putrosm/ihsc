Humanitarian Action Framework (HAF): Strengthening Health Workers' Capacity in Humanitarian Crisis Response

Humanitarian Action Framework (HAF): Penguatan Kapasitas Tenaga Kesehatan dalam Respons Krisis Kemanusiaan

NewsKabar IHSC EditorialRedaksi IHSC 11 August 202611 Agustus 2026

Profesional Training Center (PTC), in collaboration with the Indonesian Humanitarian Study Center (IHSC), is holding an online training titled "Humanitarian Action Framework (HAF): Strengthening Health Workers' Capacity in Humanitarian Crisis Response." The training is accredited "B" by the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia, No. HK.02.02/F/1381/2025.

Profesional Training Center (PTC) bekerja sama dengan Indonesian Humanitarian Study Center (IHSC) menyelenggarakan pelatihan daring bertajuk "Humanitarian Action Framework (HAF): Penguatan Kapasitas Tenaga Kesehatan dalam Respons Krisis Kemanusiaan." Pelatihan ini terakreditasi "B" Kemenkes RI, Nomor HK.02.02/F/1381/2025.

The training runs across two days, Saturday–Sunday, 29–30 August 2026, starting at 08.00 WIB via Zoom Meeting. It brings together seven speakers covering the crisis-response cycle from issue trends, international humanitarian law, disease detection in displaced populations, cross-sector coordination, the Sphere standard, to climate and health vulnerability.

Pelatihan berlangsung dua hari, Sabtu–Minggu, 29–30 Agustus 2026, mulai pukul 08.00 WIB melalui Zoom Meeting. Kegiatan ini menghadirkan tujuh pemateri yang membahas siklus respons krisis, mulai dari tren isu, hukum humaniter internasional, deteksi penyakit pada populasi pengungsi, koordinasi lintas sektor, standar Sphere, hingga kerentanan iklim dan kesehatan.

Speakers and topics:

Pemateri dan materi:

  • Dr. H. Miftahul Munir, SKM., M.Kes., DIE — Trend and Issue of Global Humanitarian Crisis
  • dr. Putro S. Muhammad, MH, CIHL — Global Humanitarian Crisis Landscape and Basic Concepts of Humanitarian Action
  • Ogy Winenriandhika, S.H., M.M. — Implementation of International Humanitarian Law in Crisis Response in Conflict Areas
  • dr. Angie Erditha, MSciH, DTM — Early Detection and Management of Communicable Diseases in Displaced Populations Based on WHO Minimum Standards
  • Zulfahmi Ramli, SE — Cross-Sector Coordination in Humanitarian Response (UN Cluster Approach)
  • Dr. dr. Lucky Tjahjono, M.Kes. — Implementation of Sphere Standards in Health Response to Complex Humanitarian Crises
  • Dr. Dea Tania Octarina, S.Si., M.Sc. — Climate Change, Migration, and Community Health Vulnerability in the Global Humanitarian Crisis
  • Dr. H. Miftahul Munir, SKM., M.Kes., DIE — Tren dan Issue Krisis Kemanusiaan Global
  • dr. Putro S. Muhammad, MH, CIHL — Lanskap Krisis Kemanusiaan Global dan Konsep Dasar Aksi Kemanusiaan
  • Ogy Winenriandhika, S.H., M.M. — Implementasi Hukum Humaniter Internasional dalam Respons Kemanusiaan di Wilayah Konflik
  • dr. Angie Erditha, MSciH, DTM — Deteksi Dini dan Penanggulangan Penyakit Menular pada Populasi Pengungsi Berdasarkan Standar Minimum WHO
  • Zulfahmi Ramli, SE — Koordinasi Lintas Sektor dalam Respons Kemanusiaan (UN Cluster Approach)
  • Dr. dr. Lucky Tjahjono, M.Kes. — Implementasi Standar Sphere dalam Respons Kesehatan pada Krisis Kemanusiaan Kompleks
  • Dr. Dea Tania Octarina, S.Si., M.Sc. — Perubahan Iklim, Migrasi, dan Kerentanan Kesehatan Masyarakat dalam Krisis Kemanusiaan Global

Participant requirements: a health worker (Nakes) or registered assistant, holding a Satu Sehat account, willing to attend the full activity, and completing the registration form.

Syarat peserta: tenaga kesehatan (Nakes) atau Named, memiliki akun Satu Sehat, bersedia mengikuti kegiatan secara penuh, dan mengisi formulir pendaftaran.

Registration closes on 28 August 2026. Participants can register, check the registration fee, and view SKP information via forms.gle/9D67v52fUxnCMTfV7.

Pendaftaran ditutup pada 28 Agustus 2026. Peserta dapat mendaftar, memeriksa investasi pendaftaran, dan melihat informasi SKP melalui forms.gle/9D67v52fUxnCMTfV7.

HAF is designed as the foundational course in this training track. Participants who complete HAF will have the prerequisite standing to take the next-level course, the Humanitarian Action Workshop (HAW), which PTC and IHSC plan to hold at a later stage.

HAF dirancang sebagai jenjang dasar dalam rangkaian pelatihan ini. Peserta yang menyelesaikan HAF akan memenuhi prasyarat untuk mengikuti jenjang lanjutan, Humanitarian Action Workshop (HAW), yang direncanakan PTC dan IHSC pada tahap berikutnya.

For further information, contact PTC at 085 1385 18637 or email medianusabarokah@gmail.com, or follow Instagram @PTC Tuban.

Informasi lebih lanjut dapat menghubungi PTC di 085 1385 18637 atau email medianusabarokah@gmail.com, atau mengikuti Instagram @PTC Tuban.
